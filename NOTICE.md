# Third-party data

The multiplication tables shown on the pages come from the GAP package
Smallsemi 0.7.2:

- Copyright (C) 2008-2025 Andreas Distler and James D. Mitchell.
- Source: <https://github.com/gap-packages/smallsemi>
- License: GPL-3.0-or-later.

The site reads them from `research/order6/catalogue.json` of
[semibase-order6](https://github.com/nasqret/semibase-order6), which is derived
from Smallsemi. Smallsemi itself is not included in this repository.

The files in `data/` (certified bases read back from the Lean theorems, their
reduced forms, the inclusions between the varieties and the census rows) come
from the `bases-min` seal of Mikoláš Janota.
